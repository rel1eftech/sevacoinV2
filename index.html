01 · Foundation

The Seva Foundation

The mission behind SevaCoin: health and trust, built into the way care is paid for, shared and governed.

Our mission

Seva means selfless service. The Seva Foundation exists to put that idea at the centre of healthcare: care that is fair to reach, records that patients can trust, and a community that has a real say in how the system grows. SevaCoin is the tool the Foundation uses to get there.

Building healthcare equity

Too much of healthcare still depends on where you live and which paperwork reaches which desk. A shared, transparent ledger lets patients, providers and programmes work from the same trusted record, wherever they are.

What the Foundation stands on

  • Secure patient data exchangeRecords move between the people who need them, protected by advanced cryptography.
  • Wellness incentivesHealthy habits and wellness programmes can be rewarded in SevaCoin.
  • Community governanceHolders help steer the health ecosystem, rather than a single gatekeeper.

Trust by design

SevaCoin runs on a Proof-of-Stake network that uses 99% less energy than traditional mining, and confirms transactions in under three seconds worldwide. Every transfer is recorded on the ledger, so anyone can verify how funds move.

Step 01 · Getting started

Choose your wallet

A wallet is necessary for people wanting to use, trade or hold SevaCoin. Here is how to pick the right one.

What a wallet does

A wallet holds the keys that prove your SevaCoin belongs to you. It lets you see your balance, receive coins and send them anywhere in the world in under three seconds.

The three kinds of wallet

  • MobileAn app on your phone. Easiest for everyday payments and receiving rewards on the go.
  • DesktopSoftware on your computer. Good for managing larger balances from home.
  • HardwareA small physical device that keeps your keys offline. The safest choice for long-term holding.

What to check before you choose

  1. It supports the SevaCoin network.
  2. You download it only from the official source or app store listing.
  3. It gives you a recovery phrase that only you control.
  4. It lets you protect it with a PIN, password or fingerprint.
Not sure which to pick? Start with a mobile wallet. You can move your SevaCoin to a hardware wallet later as your balance grows.

Step 02 · Guide

Configure your wallet

After downloading, configure your wallet with this guide. It takes a few minutes.

Set it up step by step

  1. Install and open itChoose "Create a new wallet".
  2. Write down your recovery phraseCopy the words on paper, in order, and keep them somewhere safe and offline. Never photograph them or store them online.
  3. Confirm the phraseThe wallet asks you to re-enter some words to make sure you saved them correctly.
  4. Lock itSet a strong PIN or password, and turn on fingerprint or face unlock if your device has it.
  5. Find your receive addressTap "Receive" to see the address other people use to send you SevaCoin.
  6. Test with a small amountBefore moving a larger balance, send or receive a small amount to check that everything works.
Your recovery phrase is your wallet. Anyone who has it can take your SevaCoin. The SevaCoin team will never ask you for it, and you should never type it into a website or message.

01 · What is SevaCoin?

Secure patient data exchange

SevaCoin supports the secure exchange of patient data, so the right records reach the right people, and no one else.

Why it matters

Patient records are often spread across clinics, labs and pharmacies that cannot easily share them. Every time a record has to be re-requested, posted or re-typed, care slows down and mistakes creep in.

How SevaCoin helps

  • Protected end to endAdvanced cryptographic security protects every exchange, 24/7.
  • A trusted recordA shared, transparent ledger means everyone works from the same verifiable history.
  • Fast, anywhereConfirmations take under three seconds, wherever the patient and provider are.
  • Built for the communityHow the system grows is steered by the community, not a single gatekeeper.
In short: secure, transparent and community-driven data exchange, so care can move as fast as the people who need it.

02 · What is SevaCoin?

Wellness program incentives

SevaCoin incentivizes wellness programs, rewarding the healthy habits that keep people well.

Rewarding prevention, not just treatment

Most healthcare money is spent after someone gets sick. Wellness programmes work the other way round: they encourage the everyday choices that keep people healthy. SevaCoin gives those programmes a simple, global way to say thank you.

How it works

  1. Join a programmeTake part in a wellness programme that rewards in SevaCoin.
  2. Build healthy habitsReach the goals the programme sets.
  3. Get rewardedSevaCoin arrives in your wallet, confirmed in under three seconds.

Good for people and the planet

SevaCoin runs on Proof-of-Stake, which uses 99% less energy than traditional mining, so rewarding wellness does not cost the earth.

Community governance

SevaCoin fosters community governance in the health ecosystem. The people it serves help decide where it goes.

A say for everyone

Seva means selfless service. Community governance puts that idea into practice: instead of one company deciding everything, the community proposes, discusses and votes on how the health ecosystem grows.

How the community takes part

  • ProposeSuggest new programmes, partners and improvements.
  • DiscussJoin the conversations in our worldwide community.
  • VoteHelp decide which proposals move forward.

Transparent by design. Every transaction is recorded on the ledger, so the community can verify how funds move.
